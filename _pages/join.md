# Open positions

**We are looking for new group members with creativity and talent!**

You will have the opportunity to work at the frontier of scientific machine learning, where physics and simulation meet modern AI. You’ll be involved in identifying meaningful scientific questions, developing new architectures grounded in geometry and mechanics, and applying these tools to model complex systems where traditional methods fall short. Your work may span the design of graph-based learning algorithms, integration with finite element methods, and deployment in impactful application areas spanning engineering and science.

### Postdoc positions
Applications should be submitted via [email](mailto:ntrask@seas.upenn.edu) and should include a cover letter, a CV, and two references uploaded in one attachment. In your cover letter please make sure to explain why you want to work with us, what areas you have previously worked on, and what would like to work on in the future. Strong applicants will have experience developing numerical discretization tools at a low level, have an interest in mathematics, and have experience working with modern machine learning libraries. 

### Prospective Graduate Students
We currently advise students through the following M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs:
- [Mechanical Engineering & Applied Mechanics (MEAM)](https://www.me.upenn.edu/doctoral/)
- [Applied Mathematics and Computational Science (AMCS)](https://amcs.upenn.edu/phd-program/)
- [Scientific Computing (SCMP)](https://pics.upenn.edu/masters-science-engineering-scientific-computing/)
- [Computer and Information Science (CIS)](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/graduate/program-offerings/doctoral-program/)
- [Data Science (DATS)](https://dats.seas.upenn.edu/)
- [Materials Science (MSE)](https://mse.seas.upenn.edu/)
- [Physics](https://live-sas-physics.pantheon.sas.upenn.edu/)

Please check out the respective programs and apply to whichever fits better with your profile. Admissions are made at a department level by appointed committees. Unfortunately we cannot address all the requests we receive, but if you think you have a compelling story, with concrete links to our past or current research, please reach out!

### UPenn Undergraduates
If you are a UPenn undergraduate and are interested in getting involved in the center’s research, feel free to get in touch. ​Applications should be submitted via [email](mailto:ntrask@seas.upenn.edu) and should include a cover letter and a CV uploaded in one attachment.

On the computational end, courses in the following areas will help to get involved in the lab’s research: finite elements, probability, machine learning, scientific computing, computational fluid and solid mechanics, and high performance computing.
